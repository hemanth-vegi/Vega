HOW TO FIX VEGA (3 steps, no coding)

1. Close Antigravity. Do not ask it to change Vega any more.
2. Put this whole "vega_fix" folder INSIDE C:\CloudLab\mybot
   (so you have C:\CloudLab\mybot\vega_fix\INSTALL FIX.bat)
3. Double-click  INSTALL FIX.bat  and wait until it says "All done!"

It puts every file back in working order and keeps your diary, notes, Lab Log and settings.
Your old files are saved first in a folder called backup_before_fix_... inside mybot.

To open Vega later:
  - double-click "Vega" on your desktop, or
  - click the blue V by the clock (look under the ^ arrow), or
  - press Ctrl + Alt + Space to type to her in a small box.
When you close her window, a small box appears: "Vega is still running".

VOICE UNLOCK
  If the lock screen says voice unlock needs speechbrain/torch: double-click
  INSTALL VOICE UNLOCK.bat (in the mybot folder). It shows the download
  (300 MB to 1 GB, 5 to 15 minutes) and restarts Vega when it's done.
  Then, if the lock screen says there is no voiceprint: unlock with your PIN,
  open Settings -> PIN and lock -> "Set up my voice" and read the 4 sentences.

If anything fails: double-click "Start Vega (show errors).bat" and send Claude a screenshot.
