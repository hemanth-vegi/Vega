VEGA 2 - SETUP GUIDE
====================

Everything runs on your laptop. No Claude usage.

PART 1 - INSTALL (one time)
1. Put all these files in C:\CloudLab\mybot (the same folder as your Modelfile-full).
2. Double-click "Install Vega.bat". Wait until it says Done, then press any key.
3. Make sure the Ollama app is running (Start menu -> Ollama).
4. Double-click "Start Vega.bat". The Vega window opens.
   If nothing opens, double-click "Start Vega (show errors).bat" and send a screenshot of the error.

PART 2 - MAKE IT EASY TO OPEN
In the Vega window: Menu -> Create desktop shortcut.
Menu -> Start with Windows (tick it) so Vega opens by herself every time and your reminders always work.

PART 3 - TALK TO HER
- Type and press Enter, or press Mic, speak, and stop talking (she stops listening by herself).
- Tick "Listen for Hey Vega" to talk hands-free: say "Hey Vega", wait for the beep, then speak.
  Or say it in one go: "Hey Vega, what's the weather?"
- Say "help" to see every command.

PART 4 - USE HER FROM YOUR PHONE (Telegram)
1. Install Telegram on your phone and sign in.
2. Search for @BotFather (blue tick). Send: /newbot
3. Give it a name (e.g. Vega) and a username ending in "bot" (e.g. hemanth_vega_bot).
4. BotFather sends a token like 123456789:AAH.... Copy it.
5. In the Vega window: Menu -> Connect phone (Telegram)... -> paste the token -> OK.
6. On your phone, open your new bot and send "hi".
7. A box appears on the laptop: click Yes.
Now you can message Vega from your phone (text or voice notes) while the laptop is on.
Reminders are also sent to your phone.
Keep the token secret. Don't send passwords or bank details to Vega from your phone.

SETTINGS (Menu -> Edit settings)
- "city", "latitude", "longitude": your weather place (set to SRM Kattankulathur).
- "vm_name": your VirtualBox VM (hemanth-server1).
- "apps" and "sites": add your own, e.g.  "odysseus": "http://127.0.0.1:7000"
- "speech_rate": -10 slow to 10 fast.
Save, then Menu -> Quit Vega and open her again.

YOUR FILES (all in this folder, all private on your laptop)
facts.txt       things you told her to remember
memory.json     recent chat
reminders.json  reminders
todo.json       to-do list
diary\          one text file per day
documents\      files you want her to read
